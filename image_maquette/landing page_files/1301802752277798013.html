Project: 3D E-Commerce + Social Feed

Context

Design a light, clean, premium web application combining an e-commerce platform with immersive 3D product visualization and an integrated social feed. The experience should feel like a fusion between Apple's product pages, Shopify storefront, and a lightweight Instagram/Pinterest feed.

Style & Aesthetic

  • Style: Light & Clean, editorial feel, generous white space.

  • Mood: Premium, warm, immersive, community-driven.

  • Palette: Primary Orange (#F97316), Amber (#F59E0B), warm accents, and a clean stone-based neutral scale.

  • Typography: Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans (Weights 400 & 500).

  • Radius: 16px cards, 8px buttons/inputs, 24px badges.

Key Screens to Design

  1. Landing Page: Full-screen 3D hero, featured products, community preview.

  2. Product Catalog: Filterable grid with 3D mini-previews on hover.

  3. Product Detail: 60/40 split with a 3D viewer, hotspots, and integrated social section.

  4. Social Feed: 3-column layout (Profile sidebar, Feed with 3D product embeds, Trending).

  5. Cart & Checkout: Slide-over drawer and a clean 2-column checkout.

  6. Auth: Modern centered cards for Login/Register with social options.

UI Components

  • 3D Viewer: WebGL canvas with pill-shaped glassmorphism controls.

  • Product Card: White bg, 16px radius, 3D preview zone.

  • Post Card: Social feed items with product tag pills.

  • Buttons: Primary Orange, Ghost with orange borders.

  • Navigation: Clean topbar with search, cart badge, and avatar.
